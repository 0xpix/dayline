# Play Store Listing Draft

## App name
Dayline

## Short description
A quieter, on-device calendar for planning your day.

## Full description
Dayline is a minimal Android calendar and day planner built around one question: what matters today?

Plan timed events, recurring routines and tasks; move and resize blocks directly on the day; use optional focus cycles; keep Android calendars visible without turning your schedule into a dashboard; and place configurable Dayline widgets on your home screen.

Highlights:
- minimal Today timeline with live time and conflict awareness
- Android Calendar Provider integration with per-calendar controls
- recurring events, buffers and templates
- tasks, subtasks, priorities and schedule-to-timeline
- optional 25/5, 50/10 or custom focus cycles
- ongoing event/focus activity controls
- JSON backup/restore and ICS import/export
- configurable widgets with current/next/focus states
- system, light and OLED-dark appearance
- no Dayline account, ads or analytics SDK
- open-source under the MIT License

Dayline is designed to feel native to Android and especially at home with restrained, monochrome system themes.
